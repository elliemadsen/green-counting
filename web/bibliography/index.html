About

Each node is a text cited in the bibliographies of architecture-school syllabi collected for the Buell Center's green-counting project (2020–2026). Only texts cited in at least two syllabi appear. Node size reflects how many syllabi cite the text.

Two texts are linked when the same syllabus cites both; heavier, darker lines mean they co-occur in more syllabi. The layout is force-directed, so frequently co-cited texts pull together into neighborhoods, and colors mark communities detected from the citation links.

Covers are fetched from the Open Library API where a match exists; texts without covers (most articles and reports) are drawn as title–author labels. Hover any node or line for details.

Two clustering algorithms are available. Organic (weighted label propagation) follows how texts are actually co-cited, so a few foundational texts pull huge natural neighborhoods together while the rest fragment into many small groups — anything too small to earn its own color lands in "Other." Balanced (recursive spectral bisection) instead repeatedly splits the network along its weakest connections to force even, similarly sized groups with no leftover bucket — at the cost of occasionally cutting through a genuinely tight-knit reading group just to hit the size target.
