Visual Language

Every raster image embedded in the syllabi documents, including photos, sections, diagrams, maps, and more. Excludes logos, letterheads, and headshots. Arrange the images by year, color, or syllabus. Click any image to see its source.

Images are kept only if they're placed at least 1 inch in both dimensions on the page, aren't a thin banner or rule line, and aren't a near-solid color block — a simple filter meant to drop template graphics (letterhead, icons, dividers) while keeping project photos, renderings, and diagrams. "Color" is each image's average color. In the default By year and color view, grayscale images are grouped separately, ordered dark to light; the By color view instead sorts every image by hue, however faint, so a faintly-tinted "grayish" image lands next to true-color images that share its tint rather than off in its own group.
